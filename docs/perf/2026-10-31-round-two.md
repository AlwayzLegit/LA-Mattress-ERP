### Defect: a return refunded to original tenders leaves the order owing the refund

Order LB-11230: completed, take-with, $3,047.23 paid in full. Return one mattress line ($328.49 credit incl. tax),
refund to original tenders → RMA-LB-11230-1 completes, a `kind='refund'` cash row of −$328.49 is written. The
order now reports `balanceDueCents: 32849`: "$328.49 balance due" in the order header, the orders book and the
customer page, on a customer who owes nothing. Cause: balance is `max(0, total − Σ succeeded payments)`
(`order-math.ts balanceDueCents`, and the same SQL in ~10 controllers) and the refund counts as negative money in
while the order total still includes the returned line. Same formula will do this after an exchange whose credit
leaves as a refund. Fix needs one decision from the owner — whether a completed return reduces the order's
total (invoice net of returns) or counts as a settlement — then one shared expression used by every balance
site (orders detail/list, customers, dashboards, AR report, payment plans, public order page). Proposed as a
follow-up task.
