LA MATTRESS ERP · REDESIGN

Phase 7 — Deliveries and dispatch

A board whose controls match its view. Capacity on every day as n / 15. Drag with a handle and a drop target. Drivers get paper.
The user and the question

Dispatch at the Warehouse, and the store manager checking their customers' dates. "How full is each day, and what is not ready to load?" Every day header answers the first half with a fraction and a bar; every card answers the second with its status chip — a Waiting-on-stock card on tomorrow's truck is the phone call.

What the audit asked for

The shipped board renders five weeks but pages one week at a time; headers show a bare count; empty days have no affordance; drag is described in a sentence. Here the view has two modes — Week (the working view) and Month (the overview) — and the controls change with them. Capacity is "n / 15" with amber at 13–15 and red over. Empty days say "Schedule here". Cards have a visible handle; the drop target draws itself.

Design notes

Matas on drag: the card lifts 2px with a shadow, the origin leaves a dashed ghost, the target day gets a navy dashed outline and its fraction previews the new count ("13 → 14 / 15"). Drop animates 120ms; reduced-motion snaps.

Ive vs Forstall on the soft cap: Forstall wanted the drop refused above 15. Ive: it is a soft cap by decision; refusing it hides the truth. Jobs: allow the drop, turn the day red, and make the dispatcher say why in a one-line note that prints on the day sheet.

Chaudhri: the day sheet is designed for a clipboard in a truck cab — 14pt, stop order, phone, window, balance due in bold, one line per piece to tick.
