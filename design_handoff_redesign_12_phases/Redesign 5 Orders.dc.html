Phase 5 — Orders and order detail

Live prototype (Proto Orders.dc.html) in five states. Sort, filter, open, cancel — all work.
The user and the question

A salesperson with a customer on the phone, or a manager scanning the store's book. "Where is this order, and can I still change it?" The list answers with the status chip and the reserved bar in the same row; the slide-over answers the second half with a lock banner that says who printed the ticket and who may unlock it.

What the audit asked for

Real links in every row (the order number is an anchor; Enter opens it). A Store column and a Store filter defaulting to the signed-in store. Every column sortable with aria-sort. A written-date range. One status token set from Phase 2 instead of five grey pills. Reserved progress as a bar with the fraction beside it, not a number alone. Cancel in the destructive tier, far from the primary action, with a confirm that names the deposit.

Design notes

Forstall vs Ive on the reserved bar: Ive called it a second status. Forstall: status says what, the bar says how close — a Waiting-on-stock order at 2/3 is a phone call, at 0/3 is a PO. Jobs kept the bar with the fraction so the bar is never read alone.

Chaudhri on the slide-over: 640px, actions in one row under the title, Cancel alone on the right in red outline. History is a vertical timeline with the actor on every entry because the owner reads it to settle disputes.

Later: the brief's per-store order prefix (GL-10437). Kept SO- per your call; the Store column carries the information instead.
