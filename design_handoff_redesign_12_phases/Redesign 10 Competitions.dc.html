Sales competitions — the strip on every dashboard

One live prototype (Proto Competition.dc.html) in every state below. Six races, $100 each, all five stores, all month.
The user and the question

A salesperson between customers, glancing up. "Where am I, who is ahead, and how far?" Every card answers in its pinned row: your rank, your number, and the gap to first in the metric's own units — "$340 behind Ronnie", "2 leads behind Ronnie". The strip is a scoreboard, not a report; it lives above every role's home so the whole company reads the same numbers.

Rules made visible

Completed orders only; returns come off within 30 days; ties by net sales; prizes pay on the 5th — all in the strip footer. Sweep bonus: first on 4 of 6 cards pays $1,000, 5 of 6 pays $1,500, all 6 pays $2,000, in place of the per-card $100s; the header shows who is closest once anyone leads three. Each card prints its prize and its one-line rule. Average Ticket shows "1 sale" beside a fragile average. Least Exchanges is a fraction, and zero sales is unranked with the pinned row saying "sell one to be ranked". Day one is empty on purpose and says so without a zero.

Design notes

Ive vs Chaudhri on motion: Chaudhri wanted a rank change to slide the row and pulse the card; Ive wanted nothing. Jobs: a change that happened on your own register should be visible on your own screen — one 280ms row slide and one 800ms card flash, then still. Try "Simulate a completed sale" on 1a.

Forstall on losing: no red anywhere on the board. Last place is a rank. The only accent is navy on your own row and on the leader in TV mode.

Matas on the pace line: a hairline tick where the leader lands if today's rate holds, so second place knows whether a big week is enough. It only shows expanded; collapsed keeps top three and your row.
