Phase 4 — New Sale, the register

Every artboard below is the live prototype (Proto New Sale.dc.html) in a given state. Click into any of them.
The user and the question

A salesperson with a customer in front of them. "What do I need to finish this sale?" The answer is always the rail: the customer (or the field to find one), the total, the balance, and one large button. The items table gets the full content width so every column — including Amount, which the audit found off-screen — sits in view at 1440 and at 1280.

Sourcing defaults (from the handoff)

Add Product opens from Warehouse — warehouse. A line whose effective fulfillment is take-with follows the order's Store. Untouched lines re-source when the order's fulfillment or store changes; an auto badge shows while a value is derived and disappears once the salesperson edits it. A touched line is never moved silently — it gets an inline note. Take-with with zero stock gets its own warning copy.

Design notes

Ive vs Forstall on the rail: Ive wanted a footer so items could run edge to edge. Forstall: the customer field must be where the eye lands before the first item, and a footer puts the total behind the keyboard tray on a 1280 register. Jobs took the rail (also your call), then made Ive's point stick — the rail is 316px and holds only four things.

Chaudhri on the picker: a real dialog (role, focus trap, Escape) with From as the loudest control in the toolbar — filled accent — because the whole risk of the warehouse default is a salesperson not noticing. "At Glendale" column header follows the selection; ATP gets a hover definition.

Matas on motion: a new line arrives from the top of the table in 120ms and the total re-counts; that is the only motion on the screen.
